### Prompt 2: Risk Factors

**Prompt** 
Identify the primary risk factors disclosed by management in this 10-Q filing.

**Response**
The primary risk factors disclosed by management in this 10-Q filing include:

Economic and Geopolitical Conditions: Volatile, negative, or uncertain economic and geopolitical conditions, and their effects on clients' businesses and levels of business activity, could adversely affect results of operations 1 .

Client Demand and Technological Adaptation: The company's business depends on generating and maintaining client demand for its solutions and services. A significant reduction in demand or failure to adapt to technological changes could materially affect results of operations 1 .

Risks Related to Artificial Intelligence (AI): The development and use of AI, including advanced AI, poses risks and uncertainties that could harm the business, damage reputation, or result in legal or regulatory actions 1 .

Talent Management: Inability to match people and their skills with client demand globally or failure to attract and retain professionals with strong leadership skills may adversely affect business operations and utilization rates 1 .

Data Security and Cyberattacks: Legal, reputational, and financial risks exist from potential failures to protect client and company data from security incidents or cyberattacks 1 .

Market Competition: The highly competitive markets in which the company operates may pose challenges in maintaining effective competition 1 .

Ecosystem Partnerships and Alliances: Failure to manage relationships with ecosystem partners or establish new alliances in emerging technologies may negatively impact the business 1 .

These risk factors highlight the challenges the company faces in maintaining its operational and competitive effectiveness.

### Prompt 3: Business Operations

**Prompt** 
Highlight any significant strategic changes or business operation updates mentioned in this report.

**Response**
The report outlines the following significant strategic changes and business operation updates:

Focus on Large-Scale Transformations: Clients continue to prioritize large-scale transformations, including initiatives to become AI-ready. This reflects a strategic emphasis on leveraging AI and other advanced technologies to drive client reinvention and operational improvements 1 .

Business Optimization Program Completion: The company completed a six-month business optimization program during the first quarter of fiscal 2026, which included $308 million primarily for employee severance costs. This program was aimed at optimizing operations and resources 2 .

Growth in Managed Services and Consulting: Managed services revenue increased by 10% in U.S. dollars, while consulting revenue grew by 7% for the second quarter of fiscal 2026 compared to the same period in fiscal 2025. This indicates a strategic focus on expanding both types of work to meet evolving client demands 1 3 .

Geographic and Sectoral Revenue Growth: Revenue growth was particularly strong in the Asia Pacific region, driven by Banking & Capital Markets, Communications & Media, and Public Service sectors. Modest growth was observed in the Americas and EMEA, with sectoral highlights including Insurance, Life Sciences, and Public Service 3 .

Improved Pricing and Contract Profitability: The company experienced improved pricing in some areas of the business, which contributed to better contract profitability and overall margins 1 .

These updates reflect a strategic focus on leveraging emerging technologies, optimizing operations, and expanding both geographic and service-based revenue streams.

### Prompt 4: Management Discussion

**Prompt** 
Summarize management's analysis and the key challenges faced by the company during this period.

**Response**
Management's analysis and key challenges faced by the company during this period include the following:

Revenue Growth and Regional Performance: The company achieved revenue growth across all geographic markets, with particularly strong performance in the Asia Pacific region. However, growth in the Americas and EMEA (Europe, Middle East, and Africa) was more modest 1 .

Economic and Geopolitical Challenges: Volatile and uncertain economic and geopolitical conditions, including inflationary pressures, impacted client businesses and overall levels of activity, posing challenges for maintaining consistent growth and operations 2 3 .

Business Optimization Costs: The company completed a business optimization program, incurring $308 million in costs, primarily for employee severance. While this was aimed at improving efficiency, it represents a substantial financial impact during the period 4 .

Market Competition and Demand for Services: The highly competitive nature of the markets in which the company operates, coupled with the need to adapt to changing client demands and technological advancements, remains a significant challenge 3 .

AI and Technology Risks: The development and deployment of advanced AI technologies come with uncertainties, including reputational risks, legal concerns, and regulatory challenges, which could affect operations and client relationships 3 .

Operational Risks: Challenges such as matching employee skills with client demand, managing global workforce attrition, and maintaining high utilization rates of professionals were highlighted as risks that could affect operational efficiency .

Overall, while the company experienced growth and made strategic operational adjustments, it continued to face challenges stemming from external economic conditions, technological advancements, and internal operational dynamics.
