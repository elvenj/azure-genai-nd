### 2. Risk Factors
**Prompt:** Identify the primary risk factors disclosed by management in this 10-Q filing.

**Response:** The primary risk factors disclosed by management in the 10-Q filing include:

Economic and Geopolitical Conditions: Volatile, negative, or uncertain economic and geopolitical conditions, and their effects on clients' businesses and levels of business activity, could adversely affect Accenture's results of operations 1 .

Client Demand and Technological Adaptation: The business depends on generating and maintaining client demand for solutions and services, including adapting and expanding offerings in response to ongoing changes in technology. A significant reduction in demand or failure to respond to technological changes could materially affect results of operations 1 .

Risks Related to AI: Risks and uncertainties related to the development and use of AI, including advanced AI, could harm the business, damage the company's reputation, or lead to legal or regulatory actions 1 .

Talent Management: The inability to match people and their skills with client demand globally, or to attract and retain professionals with strong leadership skills, could materially adversely affect the business and the utilization rate of professionals 1 .

Data Security: Legal, reputational, and financial risks could arise from any failure to protect client and/or Accenture data from security incidents or cyberattacks 1 .

Competitive Market: The markets in which Accenture operates are highly competitive, and the company might not be able to compete effectively 1 .

Ecosystem Partnerships: Failure to successfully manage and develop relationships with ecosystem partners or to establish new alliances in emerging technologies could impact the business 1 .

### 3. Business Operations

**Prompt:** Highlight any significant strategic changes or business operation updates mentioned in this report.

**Response:** The significant strategic changes or business operation updates mentioned in the report include:

Business Optimization Program: Accenture completed a six-month business optimization program during the first quarter of fiscal 2026. This program primarily involved employee severance costs amounting to $308 million. The program aimed to optimize operations and improve efficiency 1 , 2 .

Focus on Large-Scale Transformations: Despite economic and geopolitical uncertainties, clients continue to prioritize large-scale transformations, including becoming AI-ready. This reflects a strategic focus on leveraging advanced technologies like AI to drive client transformations 3 .

Revenue Growth by Geographic Markets:

Americas: Revenue growth was driven by Banking & Capital Markets, Software & Platforms, and Industrials, although there was a decline in Public Service, particularly in the U.S. federal business 4 .
EMEA: Growth was led by Insurance, Life Sciences, and Public Service, with significant contributions from the United Kingdom and Italy 4 .
Asia Pacific: Strong growth was observed in Banking & Capital Markets, Communications & Media, and Public Service, driven by Japan and Australia 4 .
Revenue Growth by Industry Groups: Revenue growth was particularly strong in Communications, Media & Technology, and Financial Services, while Health & Public Service experienced a slight decline 3 .

Managed Services and Consulting: Managed services showed stronger growth compared to consulting, reflecting a strategic emphasis on long-term, multi-year contracts 3 , 5 .

Pricing Improvements: The company experienced pricing improvements in some areas of its business, which contributed to better contract profitability and margins 3 .

These updates highlight Accenture's strategic focus on operational efficiency, leveraging advanced technologies, and targeting growth in specific markets and service areas.
